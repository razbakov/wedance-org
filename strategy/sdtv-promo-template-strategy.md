## Impact on Promo Sales

The template is a **sales weapon**, not just an internal tool.

1. **Client sees a finished plan before paying** — reduces "what will I get?" anxiety. The biggest objection in promo sales is vagueness. Template kills it.
2. **Faster proposals** — input 5 fields, get a content plan. Proposal time drops from hours to minutes.
3. **Higher perceived value** — structured plan looks professional, justifies price. EUR 500 feels cheap when the client sees 20+ planned touchpoints.
4. **Upsell clarity** — the plan naturally shows gaps: "You have 12 artists but only 1-month package — that covers 4 posts. Upgrade to 3-month to cover all."

---

## Scalability

One template serves N festivals. The 8 content blocks are universal across dance events. Variable inputs (artist count, price dates, shows) make each output unique enough.

**Scale path:** Template (manual) -> Spreadsheet with formulas -> Automated generator (Viktor, later).

Do NOT automate first. Validate the template with 3-5 real festivals manually. Then automate what works.

---

## Risk: Sameness

Real concern. Mitigation:

- Template defines STRUCTURE (what blocks, when, how many), not CREATIVE (visuals, tone, copy angle)
- Each festival's unique inputs (artists, dates, shows, promotions) make every plan different in substance
- Creative execution stays manual and human — Wife/SMM Lead owns visual identity per client
- Add one "custom block" slot per plan for festival-specific content (venue reveal, city guide, community spotlight)

**Bottom line:** McDonald's uses the same kitchen system for every location. Customers see different menus. Same principle.

## Standard Promo Blocks (Fixed Template)

All blocks are optional — quantity can be 0. But the STRUCTURE is always the same.

| # | Block | Variable | Source | Frequency |
|---|-------|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Artist announcements | Number of artists | SDTV | 1 per artist, spread evenly |
| 2 | Artist lineup poster | — | Client | 1x/month (updated as artists confirm) |
| 3 | Festival schedule reveal | — | Client | Once, ~2 weeks before |
| 4 | Show/concert announcements | Number of shows | SDTV | 1 per show |
| 5 | Bootcamp/workshop announcements | Number of bootcamps | SDTV | 1 per bootcamp |
| 6 | Holiday promos | Auto-detected from dates | SDTV | Automatic (Christmas, NY, Valentine's, etc.) |
| 7 | Price increase alerts | Number of price dates | SDTV | 2 per date (warning + day of) |
| 8 | Giveaway/contest | 0 or 1 | SDTV | 3 posts (announce + remind + winner) |
| 9 | 7-day countdown | Always if enabled | SDTV | 7 posts, varied formats |

**Variable inputs from festival:**
- Festival date
- Promo start date
- Number of artists (0-30)
- Number of shows (0-10)
- Number of bootcamps/workshops (0-10)
- Price increase dates (comma-separated)
- Include: giveaway, holidays, schedule, countdown (checkboxes)

**Rule:** number of each block can be 0 — it simply won't generate posts for that block. This is how Shows, Bootcamps, Giveaways work — they're optional by default.

---

## What NOT To Do

- Do not over-engineer the template before testing it with real clients
- Do not automate before manual validation (3-5 uses minimum)
- Do not make it rigid — keep one flex slot per plan
- Do not share the template itself with clients — share the OUTPUT (their personalized plan)
