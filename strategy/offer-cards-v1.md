## Strategic Positioning Rule (added 2026-04-27)

**SDTV still sells festival coverage.** Coverage — это сильнейшая часть offer-а. Поправка касается framing-а, не scope-а.

Coverage is **not** sold as commodity videography:
- ❌ "we film your event"
- ❌ "we deliver X videos"
- ❌ "we make reels"
- ❌ "we do aftermovie"

Coverage **is** sold as **official media coverage with promotional and positioning purpose**:
- ✅ to show the real atmosphere
- ✅ to prove the level of dancers and artists
- ✅ to make the festival look active and desirable
- ✅ to create social proof
- ✅ to give organizer assets for current and next-edition promotion
- ✅ to support ticket sales windows
- ✅ to keep the festival visible after the event

### Core formula

> **Coverage is what we create.**
> **Visibility is what the client buys.**
> **Positioning is why it becomes valuable.**

### 3-layer mental model

| Layer | What | Who buys it |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Production** | Coverage — capture, edit, deliverables | Operationally needed |
| **Distribution** | Reach via SDTV channels + artist/dancer network | What client actually wants |
| **Positioning** | How the market perceives the festival | Why it's worth paying for |

### Sales conversation elevation

Client может зайти через *"нам нужна coverage"* — это OK, even normal entry. **Sales job:** elevate conversation:
- from "coverage" → to "coverage that helps the festival look stronger and sell the next edition better"
- from "how many videos" → to "what perception shift do you want"

The client's "how many videos" question is a buying signal — answer it, but always reframe up.

### Old framing vs SDTV framing

| Old weak framing | Stronger SDTV framing |
|------------------|----------------------|
| "We cover your festival." | "We cover your festival in a way that makes the market feel the energy, trust the event, and want to be part of the next edition." |
| "We make reels." | "We create festival media that makes dancers want to come back next year." |
| "We do aftermovie." | "We turn the live atmosphere into proof that drives next-year tickets." |

## Architecture rules (8 commandments)

1. **Do not sell deliverable count as anchor.** Deliverables/coverage are listed under "What SDTV does" — but never as the price anchor. Coverage IS in the offer; it's just framed as production engine for visibility/positioning, not as commodity. (See Strategic Positioning Rule above.)
2. **Each card built around 8-block structure** (see template below).
3. **Each card covers some subset of pre-event / live / post-event / next-edition pipeline.** Higher tier = more phases.
4. **Diagnostic question in every card:** *"Do you feel your festival looks online as strong as it feels in real life?"* — это активный фильтр, не decoration.
5. **Boundaries explicit.** Each card has "What's NOT included" and "Best-fit signals + Anti-fit signals."
6. **Upgrade path always shown.** Where this offer leads next.
7. **Pricing built on 8 axes** (Marco M2): event size · ticket value · growth ambition · relationship warmth · urgency window · SDTV distribution scope · pre/live/post scope · annual vs one-edition value. **Not** "price per video."
8. **Sales language from ICP Section 13.** Visibility / market perception / make festival feel alive / next-edition desire. Not "we make videos."

## 🟢 SDTV Visibility Boost (internal: Entry)

> **Coverage role:** Coverage **не main component** этой двери. Опирается в основном на existing material organizer-а + light selected capture от SDTV. Coverage может быть added как scope upgrade, тогда conversation естественно лезет в Media Partnership.

### 1. Problem
Festival неплохой в реальной жизни, но online выглядит тише, чем должен. Promo рассеян, contento приходит запоздало, ticket sales медленнее чем хотелось бы. Нет момента "о, this looks like something I shouldn't miss."

### 2. Desired outcome
В ближайшие недели — festival ощущается **активным, желанным, шумящим** в social. Дансоры пересылают друг другу. Tickets двигаются быстрее. Команда festival не нанимает SMM-агентство, не жжёт время на постинг.

### 3. Why now
- Ticket sales отстают от прошлогоднего темпа на этом же этапе
- Конкурентные festivals сейчас активнее в feed
- Window to fix urgency (30-90 дней до event-а) закрывается
- Material для distribution есть — он просто не упакован

### 4. What SDTV does
Берём самый сильный материал festival-а (твой существующий + selected capture от нас) и **запускаем его через SDTV-аудиторию** (500K IG · 550K FB · 130K YT). Festival попадает в нашу ленту, истории, recap. Тебе не надо нанимать SMM-команду, согласовывать posting calendar или гнаться за trends — мы уже там.

### 5. Pipeline value

**Pre-event (когда applicable):**
- Selected promo content на SDTV-каналах
- Collab announcement post
- Story takeover / feature day

**Live event:**
- Selected social-dance / artist clip capture (limited day count)
- 1 daily story drop

**Post-event:**
- 1 recap reel
- 1 SDTV-channel feature

**Next-edition:** не входит (upgrade в Media Partnership)

### 6. Boundaries (what's NOT included)

- Annual planning

## 🟡 Festival Media Partnership (internal: Core)

> **Coverage role:** Это **main coverage offer.** SDTV covers festival officially и turns coverage into promo, social proof, recap assets, next-edition content. Coverage = central, framing = "official media coverage with promotional and positioning purpose."

### 1. Problem
Festival вырос — больше attendees, больше artists, бóльшие ставки. Но visibility-машина за ним не успевает. Нет clear promo-календаря под ticket-фазы. Live event снимают, но контент уходит без strategy. После event — momentum gas теряется через 2 недели. Каждый год начинается с нуля.

### 2. Desired outcome
SDTV становится **частью growth engine festival-а**, а не очередным vendor-ом. Ticket cycle поддержан content-ом. Live event documented strategically. Recap orchestrated. К next edition есть готовые assets и audience.

### 3. Why now
- Festival вырос — нужна visibility, соответствующая масштабу
- Conkurrents подняли planku в social
- Без structured cycle между editions — каждый год sales начинается с холода
- Available window 3–4 месяца до event-а закрывается

### 4. What SDTV does
Полный media-цикл вокруг одной edition. Мы синхронизируем content с твоими ticket-фазами, привозим команду на event, документируем strategically (не "снимаем красиво", а **снимаем то, что продаёт next year**), и в первые 4 недели после event-а у тебя готовый recap + aftermovie + content для next edition.

### 5. Pipeline value

**Pre-event (3–4 месяца):**
- Promo calendar по ticket phases (early bird → general → final push)

- Collab launches
- Final-push urgency campaign

**Live event:**

**Post-event (первые 4 недели):**
- Recap reel (premium edit)
- Aftermovie (3–5 min)
- SDTV channel feature campaign
- Artist/dancer reposts orchestrated
- Content archive delivered

**Next-edition (1–3 мес после event-а):**
- Teaser content
- Waitlist / early-bird opener
- 1 strategy call для next-year promo

## 🔴 Annual Festival Positioning Partner (internal: Premium)

> **Coverage role:** Premium coverage included **across the full visibility cycle.** Festival не просто covered once — coverage становится топливом для year-round positioning. Multi-edition organizers получают консистентное media across events + content asset library.

### 1. Problem
Festival уже большой, статусный, известный. Но между editions — vacuum. Год работаешь над одним event, потом 6 месяцев тишина. Audience уходит. Artists забывают. Repeat-attendees не получают reason возвращаться раньше, чем за месяц до next edition. Visibility-cycle не существует — есть только event-spike и долина после.

### 2. Desired outcome
SDTV становится **частью identity** festival-а, не media supplier. Год живёт как continuous narrative: pre-event campaign → live coverage → post-event recap → cross-edition content → next-year teaser → next pre-event. Festival присутствует в feed audience круглый год. Артисты тегаются и репостят без напоминаний. Next-edition tickets продаются на 30-50% быстрее.

### 3. Why now
- Festival вырос до уровня где single-event work недостаточно
- Multi-edition organizer — тратится ресурс на каждый event как первый
- Конкуренты с annual SMM look stronger даже если event у них слабее
- Year-round content требует partner, который понимает audience и language scene-а
- Strategic move: opt for partner before competitor festival берёт SDTV на эксклюзив

### 4. What SDTV does
Year-round visibility cycle. Quarterly strategic calls. Pre-event campaigns 6-9 месяцев до каждого event. Full team onsite. Premium aftermovie + multi-format derivatives. Между editions — sustained content support, archive activation, seasonal community engagement. Annual visibility plan refresh каждый Q4. SDTV становится частью команды festival, не vendor.

### 5. Pipeline value

**Annual planning (12-month cycle):**
- Quarterly strategic call (4×/year)
- Content calendar synced с ticket-phase milestones
- Cross-event content reuse strategy

**Pre-event (per edition, 6–9 месяцев):**

- Multi-format content (reels, stories, posts, YT, FB)
- Artist content series
- Early-bird + final-push campaigns

**Live event (per edition):**
- Full SDTV team onsite
- Dedicated Kirill presence где strategic
- Multi-camera setup
- Photography
- Live story orchestration
- Backstage exclusive

**Post-event (per edition):**
- Premium aftermovie
- Multi-format recap derivatives
- Artist-targeted recap series
- Multi-week SDTV channel campaign
- Press-style case study

**Between editions:**
- Monthly content support
- Content archive activation (resurface gems)
- Community engagement seasonal posts
- Guest features в SDTV channel

**Next-edition prep:**
- Strategic re-launch
- Waitlist orchestration
- Annual visibility plan refresh

## Cross-tier rules

### Pricing methodology (Marco M2 — 8 axes, NOT video count)

For Kirill K4 input, Marco anchors pricing using these dimensions per offer:

1. **Event size** — attendees scale
2. **Ticket value** — average ticket × expected attendees = topline
3. **Growth ambition** — they target +10% or +50% next year?
4. **Relationship warmth** — cold (full price) vs warm (-10%) vs whale (-15-20%)
5. **Urgency window** — 60-90 days before event = urgency premium
6. **SDTV distribution scope** — single channel feature vs multi-channel campaign
7. **Pre/live/post scope** — sub-set vs full pipeline
8. **Annual vs one-edition value** — single = sticker price, annual = volume discount

Marco prepares 3 anchor data-points per tier referencing:
- What dedicated SMM agencies charge per festival
- What dedicated video studios charge
- What Kirill currently charges existing roster

Kirill K4 = "выше / ниже / норм" + reason. No final commit until Kirill confirms.

### Discount governance
- **No random discount** without structural reason (commitment / volume / referral)
- **Multi-year:** -15% year 2, -20% year 3+
- **Whale referral:** €500 credit
- **Multi-event same year:** triggers Annual Positioning tier
- **"You're my friend, give me half"** → reframe through offer trade-down (smaller scope at smaller price), NOT discount on full scope

### Scope creep protection
"What's NOT included" lists explicit. Client requests outside scope → either:
1. Add as line-item upsell with separate price
2. Decline and clarify
3. Flag to Marco if structurally important to current tier

### Diagnostic question — required pre-pitch
**"Do you feel your festival looks online as strong as it feels in real life?"**

Their answer drives the tier:
- "It's fine" → likely not ICP, deprioritize
- "Could be better" → Visibility Boost or Media Partnership
- "We feel undersold online" → Media Partnership or Annual
- "We want it to look bigger" → Annual Positioning Partner
