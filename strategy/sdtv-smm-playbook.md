## 1. Caption Formulas by Content Type

### A. Dance Video (Reel / YouTube)

```
[Dancer names] bringing [style] magic at [Festival] [emoji-optional]

[1 sentence about the moment — energy, connection, crowd reaction]

Full video on our channel — link in bio.

#socialdancetv #[style] #[festival] #socialdancing
```

Example:
> Maria & Carlos bringing bachata magic at Mambo Nights 2026
>
> That connection when the music takes over and everything else disappears.
>
> Full video on our channel — link in bio.
>
> #socialdancetv #bachata #mambonights #socialdancing

### B. Stories

```
[Short hook — 3-5 words max]
[Tag relevant people/festival]
```

Examples:
- "This. Right. Here." + tag
- "When the music hits different" + tag
- "Saturday night energy" + festival tag

### C. Festival Promo / Warmup

```
[Festival] is [X days] away.

[1 line about what makes it special — lineup, vibe, location]

[Social proof: "We've covered this event for X years" OR "X artists confirmed"]

Are you going? [CTA: comment, DM, link]

#[festival] #socialdancetv #[city] #[style]
```

### D. Live / On-Site Content

```
Live from [Festival] [emoji-optional]

[What's happening right now — 1 energetic sentence]

Stay tuned for more — we're filming all [weekend/night].

#[festival] #live #socialdancetv
```

### E. Post-Festival / Proof / Nostalgia

```
[Festival] 2026 — what a ride.

[1-2 sentences: highlight moment, stat, or feeling]

[X] videos coming soon. Who wants to relive it?

Tag someone who was there.

#[festival] #throwback #socialdancetv #[style]
```

### F. Carousel / Photo Post

```
[Festival] in [X] frames.

[Slide descriptions or let photos speak]

Which moment hits hardest? Tell us below.

Photos by @[photographer] | Video @socialdancetv

#[festival] #socialdancetv #dancephotography
```

## 3. Promo Warmup Sequence (7-Day Countdown)

| Day | Format | Content | Tone |
|-----|--------|---------|------|
| -7 | Post (Reel/Carousel) | "7 days to [Festival]" — lineup reveal or past highlights reel | Excitement, anticipation |
| -6 | Story | Poll: "Who's going?" + countdown sticker | Interactive, community |
| -5 | Story | Artist spotlight — short clip or photo of confirmed artist | Authority, insider info |
| -4 | Post | "What to expect" — 3-5 things that make this festival special | Informative, FOMO |
| -3 | Story | Behind-the-scenes: packing gear, travel prep | Personal, relatable |
| -2 | Story | "Last chance to grab passes" (if applicable) + link | Urgency, CTA |
| -1 | Reel | Hype video: best moments from last edition, text overlay "TOMORROW" | Peak energy, FOMO |

**Post-arrival (Day 0):** "We're here" story with venue/city shot.

## 4. Phase-Based Content Plan Template

### Pre-Event (2-4 weeks before)

| Week | Posts | Stories | Focus |
|------|-------|---------|-------|
| -4 | 1 | 2 | Announcement, lineup, "we'll be there" |
| -3 | 1 | 2 | Artist spotlight, past highlights |
| -2 | 1 | 3 | Countdown, polls, engagement |
| -1 | 2 | 5 | 7-day warmup sequence (see above) |

Tone: building anticipation, insider knowledge, FOMO.

### On-Site (during event)

| Time | Format | Content |
|------|--------|---------|
| Arrival | Story | "We're here" + venue shot |
| Each session | Story (2-3) | Quick clips, energy, crowd |
| Daily | 1 Reel | Best moment of the day/night |
| Shows | Story + Reel | Artist performances, reactions |
| Social dancing | Story | Tag dancers, raw moments |

Tone: real-time energy, you-are-here, FOMO for those not there.
Volume: 5-10 stories/day, 1 reel/day.

### Post-Event (1-2 weeks after)

| Day | Format | Content |
|-----|--------|---------|
| +1 | Story | "Thank you [Festival]" + best photo |
| +2 | Reel | Recap / best-of montage (60s) |
| +3-5 | Posts | Individual dance videos (start publishing) |
| +7 | Post | "All videos are live" announcement |
| +14 | Story | "Missing [Festival]?" nostalgia poll |

Tone: proof, nostalgia, satisfaction, "look what happened."

### Between Events (maintenance mode)

| Frequency | Format | Content |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| 2x/week | Reel | Dance videos from archive or recent events |
| 3x/week | Story | Polls, reposts, dance memes, community engagement |
| 1x/month | Post | Festival calendar update or "where we're going next" |

Tone: community, warmth, keeping the page alive without events.

## 5. AI Quick-Draft Prompts

### A. Video Caption Generator

```
Write a short Instagram caption for a social dance video.
Dancers: [names]. Style: [bachata/salsa/kizomba].
Festival: [name]. Mood: [energetic/romantic/playful/intense].
Include 1 sentence about the dance, then "Full video — link in bio."
Add 4-6 relevant hashtags. Keep it under 150 words. Warm, not corporate.
```

### B. Story Poll Generator

```
Create 3 Instagram story poll questions for a [pre-event/during/post-event]
context. Festival: [name]. Style: [bachata/salsa].
Each poll has exactly 2 options. Keep questions short (under 8 words).
Make them fun and engaging, not corporate.
```

### C. Festival Promo Text

```
Write a promotional Instagram post for [Festival name] in [city].
Dates: [dates]. Highlight: [lineup/location/vibe].
SDTV is the official media partner. Mention our coverage.
Include social proof. Add a CTA. 4-6 hashtags.
Tone: excited insider, not ad copy. Under 200 words.
```

### D. Recap / Nostalgia Post

```
Write a post-festival recap caption for [Festival name].
Key stats: [X videos filmed, X dancers, X nights].
Mention 1-2 highlight moments. Ask followers to tag someone who was there.
Tone: warm, grateful, "what a ride." Under 150 words.
```

### E. Engagement Post

```
Write an engagement-focused Instagram post for a social dance community.
Topic: [lead/follow dynamics | festival tips | dance style debate | travel].
Format: question or "this or that" to drive comments.
Tone: friendly, community, like a group chat. Under 100 words.
```

## Usage Notes

- **Never sound corporate.** SDTV is the cool friend at the festival, not a brand.
- **Tag people.** Every video post tags the dancers. Every festival post tags the organizer.
- **Stories > Posts for real-time.** Posts are for permanent content. Stories are for energy.
- **Polls drive reach.** Instagram rewards interactive stories. Use polls on every story day.
- **Consistency > perfection.** A decent post on time beats a perfect post 3 days late.
