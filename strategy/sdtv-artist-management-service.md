# SDTV Artist Management Service — Design Spec

Created: 2026-04-11
Owner: Marco (strategy) + Luna (playbooks)
Status: DRAFT — awaiting founder review

---

## 1. Service Name Options

1. **Artist Activation** — emphasizes action, not passive management
2. **Artist Promo Engine** — ties directly to the value (promo output)
3. **Artist Media Coordination** — neutral, professional

Recommended: **Artist Activation** — short, clear, differentiating.

---

## 2. Client-Facing Description

> Your artists already have audiences. We turn that into ticket sales.
>
> SDTV coordinates your festival's artists to publish structured promotional content — stories, reels, posts — timed to your event calendar. Each artist receives clear, actionable briefs designed to amplify your reach through their personal audiences. You get organized promo from 5-15 artists instead of hoping they post on their own.

---

## 3. Tier Placement

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Part of Tier 3 | Increases Tier 3 value, justifies premium | Can't sell standalone |
| Standalone add-on | Flexible, sellable with any tier | Adds complexity to pricing |

**Recommendation:** Hybrid.
- **Included in Tier 3** (full media partnership) — 3-5 artists
- **Available as add-on** for Tier 1/2 — per-artist pricing
- **Standalone** for festivals that don't need video — promo-only deal

This aligns with the strategic shift: promo is the core product.

## 4. Pricing

### Per-artist pricing logic

| Factor | Reasoning |
|--------|-----------|
| SDTV creates briefs | 30 min per artist per event |
| SDTV coordinates delivery | Follow-up, approval, timing |
| Artist's audience activated | 2K-50K+ reach per artist |
| Festival gets organized promo | vs. zero without coordination |

### Price ranges

| Package | Artists | Price (EUR) | Per artist |
|---------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Starter | 3 artists | 300 | 100 |
| Standard | 5 artists | 450 | 90 |
| Full roster | 10 artists | 800 | 80 |
| Custom (15+) | By quote | ~70/artist | negotiable |

### Included in Tier 3

Tier 3 already includes positioning. Artist Activation for 3-5 artists is included. Additional artists at add-on rate (EUR 80/artist).

### Revenue estimate

Average festival has 8-12 artists. If 50% of Tier 2/3 clients add this:
- 10 deals/year x 5 artists x EUR 90 = EUR 4,500 incremental revenue

## 6. Artist Task Templates by Phase

### Pre-Event (2-4 weeks before)

| Task | Format | Artist action | Value to festival |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| "I'm going to [Festival]" announcement | Story + Post | Share with personal audience | Early awareness, social proof |
| Festival lineup reveal | Story repost | Repost festival's lineup graphic | Audience sees who's coming |
| "What should I teach?" poll | Story poll | Engage their followers about festival | Creates anticipation, drives ticket interest |

### On-Site (during event)

| Task | Format | Artist action | Value to festival |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Arrival story | Story | "Just landed at [Festival]!" | FOMO, real-time energy |
| Workshop promo | Story + tag | Film 15-sec teaser, tag festival | Drives workshop attendance |
| Social dance shoutout | Story/Reel | Tag partner + festival | Extends reach through tagged people |

### Post-Event (1-2 weeks after)

| Task | Format | Artist action | Value to festival |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Highlight reel share | Post/Reel | Share SDTV video with festival tag | Proof, nostalgia, festival authority |
| "Thank you [Festival]" post | Post | Personal reflection + tag | Warm closure, strengthens relationship |
| "See you next year" story | Story | Forward-looking, renewal signal | Primes audience for next edition |

## 7. How It Shows in Portal & CRM

### Client Portal
- Listed as deliverable: "Artist Activation — [N] artists"
- Status tracking: brief_sent → content_created → published → reported
- Performance card with aggregate reach numbers

### CRM (Monday.com)
- Sub-items under the deal: one per artist
- Status column: briefed / confirmed / posted / reported
- Linked to festival promo timeline

### Admin Panel
- Add as deliverable via "+ Video+Photo" or "+ Full set" (add to preset list)
- Or manual add: "Artist Activation" deliverable

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Artists don't post | Follow-up protocol, gentle reminders, SDTV relationship leverage |
| Low quality posts | Provide templates + examples, not just instructions |
| Festival doesn't see value | Include reach numbers in report, compare vs. festival's own reach |
| Too much coordination time | Standardize briefs, batch-send, limit to 10 artists max per event |
