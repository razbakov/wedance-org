## 1. PRICING & LADDER — Three monetization archetypes compared

The question is not what should we charge for a dancer service. The question is: which monetization SHAPE produces the best unit economics, time-to-revenue, capacity ceiling, compounding, and brand fit for the 100k+ insecure-dancer pain pool?

### Archetype A — Productized Ladder (current revised artist sub-ladder)

Shape: Profile Fix EUR 200 -> Image Starter EUR 590-790 -> Signature Presence EUR 1500-3500 -> Retainer EUR 600-1500/mo. Transactional. Each tier is a one-shot purchase.

Unit economics:
- Profile Fix EUR 200: ~3-4h Kirill = EUR 50-65/hour. Below SDTV opportunity cost. Loss-leader unless converts upward at 30%+ within 60 days. Realistically converts at 10-15%.
- Image Starter EUR 790: ~11.5h labor (per funnel-eval) = EUR 69/hour. Marginal.
- Signature Presence EUR 2400: ~24h labor = EUR 100/hour. Healthy.
- Retainer EUR 800/mo: ~6h/month = EUR 133/hour. Best per-hour, but capacity-killing — every retainer is permanent founder time.
- Hours per EUR 1k revenue: ~14h. Linear. Always.

Time-to-first-revenue: 2-4 weeks.
Capacity ceiling: ~3 Starters/month + 1 Signature/quarter = EUR 30-40k/year MAX before founder time leaks from SDTV.
Compounding: Weak. One-shot deliveries. Referral rate from EUR 200 Profile Fix near zero. EUR 790 Starter: ~10-15% over 12 months. No community effect.
Brand fit: EUR 200 Profile Fix CHEAPENS BB premium positioning. Anyone Googling brandbureau.studio after Profile Fix sees EUR 18k Signature on same site and disconnects. Net negative for BB master brand.
Verdict: Functional but not scalable. Not the right vertical-builder.

### Archetype B — Cohort Program (Stop Being Invisible)

Shape: 8-week cohort, EUR 890 (or EUR 690 founding). 8-12 dancers per run. 2-3 runs/year. Weekly group call (90 min), private community, 1 manual Profile Check per dancer, group critiques, optional BCN photo days.

Unit economics:
- Per cohort revenue: EUR 690-890 x 10 = EUR 6900-8900 per run.
- Delivery time per cohort:
  - 8 weekly group calls x 90 min = 12h Kirill
  - Prep + slides + framework: 8h Kirill (front-loaded, reusable)
  - 10 individual Profile Checks: 5h Kirill
  - 4 group critique async reviews: 8h Kirill + 4h Wife
  - Cohort admin: 4h Wife
  - Total: ~37h Kirill + 8h Wife = 45h
- Hours per EUR 1k revenue: ~5h. Three times better than productized.
- 80% of prep is REUSABLE for cohort 2, 3, 4. Cohort 2 onwards drops to ~25h Kirill / cohort.

Time-to-first-revenue: 10-12 weeks (waitlist build + cohort start). Slower than productized.
Capacity ceiling: 3 cohorts/year x 12 dancers x EUR 890 = EUR 32k/year on ~75h Kirill. Plus alumni upgrades 20-30% within 12 months = additional EUR 8-15k. Total ceiling: EUR 40-50k/year on ~120h Kirill. EUR 333-417/hour.
Compounding: STRONG. Each cohort produces 8-12 alumni in parallel (built-in social proof), 8-12 before/after pairs with permission baked into the program, community word-of-mouth, alumni referrals (cohort dynamics drive 20-40% rates), and marketing material density that productized never matches.
Brand fit: STRONGER than productized. Cohort is what category-leaders run (Korke Academy, Sara Lopez Bachata Body, Daniel y Desiree). Premium signal stronger than EUR 200 Profile Fix. Does NOT muddy BB master brand IF priced correctly (EUR 890 below BB Opening EUR 6500 but ABOVE the Profile Fix that currently cheapens BB).
Verdict: This is the right shape.

### Archetype C — Membership (EUR 49-99/mo)

Shape: Monthly subscription. Content drops, monthly call, async critique queue, community access.

Unit economics:
- EUR 79/mo x 100 members = EUR 7900/mo = EUR 95k/year ARR. Sounds great.
- Delivery cost:
  - Monthly content drop: 12h/month locked recurring
  - Monthly call: 2.5h/month
  - Async critique queue (1 per quarter per member): 11h/month
  - Community management: 4h/month Wife
  - Total: ~30h/month founder team locked recurring. Part-time job, forever.
- Hours per EUR 1k: ~3.8h. Best on paper.
- BUT: churn 8-12%/month is realistic for unbranded membership. To MAINTAIN 100 members you must SELL 8-12 new every month, indefinitely. Sales effort never ends.

Time-to-first-revenue: 2-3 weeks.
Capacity ceiling: LOOKS unbounded but bounded by churn-replacement sales. Realistic 150-200 members = EUR 142-190k ARR, requires 25-40h/month sales+content indefinitely. Becomes a business not a side bet. Kirill cannot run this AND SDTV.
Compounding: Medium. Members are warm but DO NOT self-organize like cohorts. Diluted testimonials (consuming, not transformed). Weak referrals (no graduation moment).
Brand fit: Net negative for premium positioning. EUR 79/mo says content not transformation. Contradicts EUR 18k BB Signature.
Verdict: Trap for this operator. Looks lucrative, generates founder time bondage, weakens premium, requires perpetual hustle. Skip.

### Comparison summary

| Dimension | A. Productized | B. Cohort | C. Membership |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|---------------|
| Hours / EUR 1k | 14h | 5h (3-4h after cohort 1) | 3.8h theoretical / 6-8h real |
| Time-to-first-revenue | 2-4 weeks | 10-12 weeks | 2-3 weeks |
| Annual revenue ceiling | EUR 30-40k | EUR 40-50k | EUR 142-190k (trap) |
| Founder time at ceiling | 200+ hours | 120 hours | 350+ hours/yr recurring |
| Compounding | Weak | Strong | Medium |
| Brand fit (BB master) | Negative | Positive | Negative |
| Founder lock-in | None | Bounded (cohort window) | Permanent |
| Best fit for this operator | No | YES | No |

The cohort wins on every dimension that matters to a 4-active-initiatives founder.

## 2. RECOMMENDED PRODUCT MIX — what launches first

Do not launch the cohort first. Launch the FEEDER first, then the cohort, then watch the alumni upsell.

### Mix to launch (3 products, in order)

Product 1 — SDTV Artist Profile Check (FREE, capped 5/week)

KEEP. Already in active sprint. Qualifier and audience-warming engine. Capped at 5/week prevents founder burnout. Templated 5-section deliverable. AI-assisted, human-polished. Stays exactly as sprint defines it.

Role: top of funnel, free entry, demand-signal capture, waitlist seed for cohort.

Product 2 — Artist Image Starter (EUR 790, EUR 590 founding 12)

KEEP from current sub-ladder, single price (EUR 790), no range. In-between transactional offer for dancers NOT ready for 8 weeks but want a tangible upgrade.

Scope: positioning call (45 min) + bio rewrite + 1 BCN mini-shoot (or async if not BCN) + 5-10 portraits + 3 short videos + 14-day posting plan.

Role: middle option, captures impatient dancers, generates testimonials, downsell/warm-up for cohort applicants who do not get accepted into next cohort.

Product 3 — Stop Being Invisible Cohort (EUR 890, EUR 690 founding) — NEW

THE CENTERPIECE. Launch September 2026.

- 8 weeks. Sept 1 - Oct 31.
- 8-12 dancers max. Apply to join.
- Weekly 90-min group call (Tuesday 7-8:30pm CET — works for European dancers globally).
- Private Telegram or Circle community.
- Each dancer: ONE individual Profile Check + ONE 30-min 1:1 mid-cohort.
- 4 group critique sessions.
- Optional add-on: 2-day BCN photo intensive in Oct, EUR 600 extra.
- Final cohort showcase: each dancer presents before / after / who I am now — content + testimonial fuel.

Outcome promise: By end of 8 weeks you have a profile that signals what you actually do, content that builds on itself, and a community of artists at your level.

What it is NOT: not a guarantee of bookings. Not becoming a star. It is identity + image clarity + community. Sell the identity transformation, not bookability. Bookability is downstream consequence, not headline.

Role: anchor revenue. Compounding asset producer. Feeder into BB Image Starter, Opening, Signature for top 10-20% of alumni who level up beyond cohort.

### What to KILL from current artist sub-ladder

1. KILL: Artist Profile Fix EUR 150-250. Loss-leader that cheapens brand. Anyone who would buy this either needs free Profile Check or EUR 790 Starter. Ladder filler with no economic justification and brand-negative positioning. Replace with cohort waitlist signup.

2. KILL: Artist Visibility Retainer EUR 600-1500/mo. Sounds nice on a tier list, no real demand validation, locks Kirill recurring time with no exit. Replace with cohort alumni community access (paid optional, EUR 39/mo, low maintenance, only for cohort graduates).

3. KILL: Signature Presence EUR 1500-3500. Awkward price — too expensive for casual dancer, too cheap to feel like BB core. Either lives as private alumni upgrade at EUR 2400 (matching BB Sprint middle-tier from funnel-eval), OR folds into BB Opening EUR 6500. My call: fold into BB Opening for non-cohort buyers, keep EUR 2400 Cohort Alumni Sprint as private upgrade option.

## 4. BRAND ARCHITECTURE — my call

### Options on the table

1. brandbureau.studio/dancers (dedicated landing on Brand Bureau site)
2. artists.brandbureau.studio (subdomain)
3. SDTV Artist Track (sub-brand co-branded with SDTV)

### My call: brandbureau.studio/dancers

Why NOT artists.brandbureau.studio:
Subdomains fragment SEO, fragment trust, add infra overhead. Subdomains signal a separate thing — but commercially we WANT cohort alumni to flow upward into BB Opening / Signature. A subdomain creates a brand wall where we want a flow.

Why NOT SDTV Artist Track:

1. B2B festival cannibalization (PRIMARY concern, established in funnel-eval). SDTV value to organizers = neutral festival media partner with 1M+ followers. If SDTV publicly co-brands a paid program selling to dancers, organizers see SDTV as a vendor monetizing their talent on the side. This is the single biggest brand risk in this venture. SDTV B2B trust takes 2-3 years to build, 1 conversation to crack. Will not budge.

2. SDTV is editorial trust, not a service brand. SDTV Artist Track sounds like SDTV is launching a course — a category SDTV has never been in. Trust transfer does not work cleanly: dancers trust SDTV for festival coverage, not for fix your career programs. Moves SDTV into category that dilutes editorial brand AND makes it look like a content arm of a service business.

3. Sub-brand creates double-tax on attention. Kirill cannot maintain 4 active brands (SDTV, BB, WeDance, Arancha) AND a 5th branded sub-product. Sub-brands need their own identity, voice, content engine, management overhead.

Why brandbureau.studio/dancers WORKS:

- One brand house. BB master brand becomes Brand Bureau Barcelona — image studio for public-facing professionals, including dancers/performers. Dancer page is a vertical, not a sub-brand.
- SEO benefit (single domain authority).
- Cohort alumni flow up to BB Opening/Signature without crossing brand walls.
- SDTV remains the editorial channel that REFERS to BB in DM only — exactly the brand-wall framing established in prior eval.
- Public copy does not say powered by SDTV. It says by Kirill Korshikov, founder of Brand Bureau Barcelona. Kirill is the trust bridge. SDTV is audience source, not co-brand.

B2B cannibalization risk under this model: LOW. Festival organizers Googling SDTV find SDTV. Festival organizers Googling Kirill find both, listed as separate ventures on his personal LinkedIn — that is the right level of cross-listing. Festival organizers do NOT see SDTV is selling artist services because SDTV editorial channels do not name Brand Bureau publicly. Path: SDTV post -> DM keyword -> Profile Check delivery -> if cohort is open, link to brandbureau.studio/dancers in PRIVATE only.

Same brand-wall logic as prior eval: editorial in SDTV (free), commercial conversion in private DM, paid product on Brand Bureau.

## 5. STOP-RULES — when to kill

90-day cohort audit window: 2026-09-01 launch to 2026-11-30 audit.

### Hard kill triggers (any one fires -> stop)

1. Cohort 1 fills <6 paying dancers by 2026-08-25. Demand signal too weak to justify cohort 2 prep.
2. Cohort 1 NPS <60% would recommend. Cohort programs are word-of-mouth; <60% means we built the wrong thing.
3. Kirill cohort delivery hours exceed 50h actual (vs 37h budgeted). Model is mis-sized, not a sustainable operator fit.
4. Wife reports cohort delivery cannibalizing SDTV festival SMM time during Sept-Oct. Festival pipeline always wins.
5. Festival organizer mentions confusion about Brand Bureau / SDTV brand split. Same canary as prior eval. One mention triggers brand-wall review.
6. Pre-cohort waitlist at 2026-08-15 has <15 names. Cohort needs 2-3x oversubscription to fill 8-12 actual seats.

### Soft triggers (audit at 2026-11-30)

7. <20% of cohort 1 alumni opt in to ongoing alumni community (paid or free). Community moat does not exist.
8. <2 testimonial-grade case studies emerge from cohort 1. Cannot market cohort 2.
9. <1 alumni upgrades into BB Opening/Signature pipeline within 6 months post-cohort. Cohort feeds BB core thesis fails.

### Pivot options if killed

- Keep Profile Check + Image Starter only. Revert to current MVP sprint. BB dancer vertical = transactional only.
- Convert cohort framework to 1:1 high-touch program at EUR 2400. Different unit economics, lower compounding.
- Sell or license the cohort framework. Curriculum + community design has resale value.

Marco audits at 2026-08-25 (pre-launch fill check), 2026-10-31 (cohort completion), 2026-11-30 (90-day audit). Recommendation goes to Kirill. Sage in the loop on day-90 (overload check). Wife in the loop because cohort delivery affects her SMM rhythm.

## 6. WHAT NOT TO DO — top 5 traps

1. Do not launch the cohort BEFORE Wife consultation closes green. Same blocker as MVP sprint. Wife co-pilots cohort delivery (image-side critique, content rhythm, photo days). Without explicit yes on 8 weeks of additional load during Sept-Oct, cohort fails on capacity, not market.

2. Do not run two cohorts back-to-back in 2026. Cohort 1 = Sept-Oct. Cohort 2 = Jan-Mar 2027 minimum. Operator needs Nov-Dec window to digest learnings, produce alumni testimonials, refill SDTV festival sales pipeline that took a hit during cohort delivery, and NOT BURN OUT. Skipping rest window is the most predictable failure mode.

3. Do not turn the cohort into a get bookings program. The promise is identity + image + community, not bookings. Bookings are downstream. The moment we sell we will get you booked at festivals we (a) overpromise, (b) cannibalize SDTV B2B by competing with festival organizers own talent decisions, (c) attract wrong cohort applicants (transactional, not transformational). Sell the identity transformation. Bookings happen when identity is right.

4. Do not add ANY paid ads in the first cohort cycle. Founding cohort fills from organic SDTV editorial + Kirill personal warm network only. Paid ads before testimonials exist is a EUR 500-2000 hole. Wait until cohort 1 closes with case studies, then test paid only for cohort 2 if organic alone undershoots.

5. Do not let the cohort displace SDTV festival sales focus during August. August is festival pre-event ramp. SDTV deals close in August for September festivals. If Kirill is in cohort prep mode in August, festival deals slip into September and revenue collapses. Cohort prep = July (curriculum + waitlist content), Cohort delivery = Sept-Oct, Festival sales = always-on but defended in August.
