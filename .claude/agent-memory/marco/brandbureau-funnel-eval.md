## 1. EVALUATION OF KIRILL FUNNEL — Hard critique

### What works (don't break)
1. **Profile Check beats free PDF.** Active intent vs. passive collection — correct call.
2. **ManyChat keyword router (ARTIST/PROFILE/GUIDE/BOOKED/SPRINT)** — proper segmentation by temperature. Saves hours.
3. **Editorial vertical inside SDTV ("Artist Image Check by SDTV")** — frames offer as service journalism, not advertising. Only sustainable angle.
4. **Native audience match.** Dancers/performers IS one of the four declared verticals. Wedge logic is correct.
5. **Starter as feeder.** Conceptually right — low-commitment proof of taste before 18k EUR.

### Where it is weak

**A. Cannibalization risk on SDTV brand (high, underestimated)**
- SDTV commercial value to organizers = "festival media partner with 1M+ followers." That is a B2B trust asset worth 5-15k EUR per event package.
- If SDTV channels run personal-brand offers to dancers, two things happen:
  1. Organizers see "SDTV is selling services to my talent" — feels like a media partner who is also a vendor on their roster. Awkward at minimum, conflict-of-interest at worst.
  2. SDTV editorial credibility softens. Audience starts seeing it as a content-marketing arm, not an editorial brand.
- **Mitigation:** keep editorial vertical strictly informational. Conversion happens in DMs (private). Brandbureau is NEVER named in public SDTV posts. Site lives at brandbureau.studio, not sdtv.com/artists. Treat SDTV as referrer, not co-brand.

**B. Funnel volume math is fragile**
- SDTV reach: 1M+ combined, but only ~5-10% are "artists who care about bookability." Realistic addressable: 50-100k.
- ManyChat keyword conversion on educational posts: 0.3-1.5% of views typically click-through to DM.
- Of those, profile-check requests with full email/WhatsApp capture: ~30-50%.
- Profile Checks to paid Starter at 750 EUR+: 5-10% in cold-warm audience. Higher (15-20%) in already-warm SDTV community, but only after 2-3 months of editorial trust.

**C. Profile Check delivery is invisible labor**
- "Manual or templated" — Kirill has not budgeted time. A real Profile Check takes 25-40 min: open IG, watch 3-5 reels, read bio, compose 4-section response.
- At 10 checks/week (low estimate during launch) that is 4-7 hours/week of founder time. Unfunded.
- **Fix:** Maximum 5 free checks/week. Templated structure (Loom + 4-section text). Anything over the cap -> "Profile Check Plus 49 EUR" to skip queue. Captures the tail without burning hours.

**D. Funnel is single-channel dependent**
- 100% of leads come from SDTV. If editorial vertical underperforms, funnel = zero. No second leg.
- Founders/doctors/coaches verticals cannot piggyback on the same engine. Need separate sources (see section 5).

**E. Starter price too low for the tier ladder**
- 400-700 EUR Starter creates a price anchor that makes 18k EUR Signature look like a 20-30x jump. People do not trust 20x ladders.
- See pricing decision (section 2) and ladder analysis (section 3).

**F. "We see amazing dancers all the time" angle has a tone risk**
- Reads as judgment-from-above ("we evaluate you"). For a community SDTV is also part of, that can land badly.
- Reframe: "We see what works on the dance floor and what works on Instagram — they are not the same skill. Most artists are great at one, missing the other."

### Risks ranked
1. **Cannibalization of SDTV B2B trust** — High. Hardest to recover.
2. **Founder time leak via free Profile Checks** — High. Easiest to fix (cap + templated).
3. **Ladder break Starter -> Signature** — High. Fix with middle-tier.
4. **Single-channel funnel** — Medium. Acceptable for v1 if Brandbureau stays as side bet.
5. **Tone of editorial framing** — Medium. Copy fix.

## 2. PRICING DECISION — Artist Image Starter

### Recommendation: **890 EUR anchor, 750 EUR early-bird (first 12 customers)**

NOT 400-700. NOT 1200. Here is why.

### Defense via unit economics

**Cost stack per Starter (mostly time, no studio):**
- 30-min positioning call: 1.5h (call + prep + notes)
- Bio rewrite: 1h
- Mini shoot (1 location, BCN, 90 min): 1.5h shooting + 1h travel = 2.5h
- Edit 5-10 portraits: 2h
- 3 short videos cut: 2h
- 14-day posting plan: 1.5h
- Delivery + 1 revision round: 1h
- **Total: 11.5h per Starter, ~80% Kirill, ~20% Wife**

**At 400 EUR:** 34 EUR/hour. Below his SDTV per-hour rate. Loss-making relative to opportunity cost.
**At 700 EUR:** 60 EUR/hour. Marginal. Does not price like premium positioning studio.
**At 890 EUR:** 77 EUR/hour. Floor of "premium taste" perception. Survives cancellation/no-show on 1 in 3 without going negative.
**At 1200 EUR:** 104 EUR/hour. Healthy, but blocks volume needed to feed Signature pipeline. Conversion drops below 5%.

### Why 890 EUR specifically
- Above the 750 EUR "premium psychological floor" — below 1k EUR is "expensive" but not "luxury bracket."
- 20x cheaper than Signature (18k EUR) — clean ratio, does not trigger 30x-40x ladder distrust.
- Round-number cleanliness: 890 prints better than 875 or 925.
- Gives room for 750 EUR early-bird ("first 12 founding clients") — creates urgency without permanent discount.

### Why early-bird matters
- First 12 are testimonial fuel — you NEED real before/after with permission to show.
- 750 EUR x 12 = 9k EUR revenue from Cohort 1. Enough to pay for Wife SMM time on Brandbureau channel + email infra.
- Sets Cohort 2 anchor at 890 EUR minimum, not at 750.

## 3. LADDER COHERENCE

### Current state
- Starter (proposed): 400-1200 EUR
- Opening: 6 500 EUR
- Signature: 18 000 EUR
- Positioning: 4 200 EUR (strategy only — sideways tier, not a step)
- Retainer: 4 800 EUR/mo (post-Signature continuation)

### Problem
**Starter (890) -> Opening (6 500) is a 7.3x jump.** Then Opening -> Signature is 2.8x. The first jump is the killer. Customers who loved Starter need a credible 2-3k EUR step before 6.5k makes sense.

### Recommended ladder

| Tier | Price | Role | Time |
|------|-------|------|------|
| Profile Check | 0 EUR (capped) / 49 EUR (skip queue) | Free entry, qualifier | 30 min |
| **Artist Image Starter** | **890 EUR** (750 early) | Feeder, taste proof | 2-week sprint |
| **Artist Image Sprint** *(NEW)* | **2 400 EUR** | Middle-tier — multi-shoot, multi-platform | 4 weeks |
| The Opening | 6 500 EUR | Studio day, full day production | 2 weeks |
| The Signature | 18 000 EUR | Full personal-brand build | 6 weeks |
| The Positioning | 4 200 EUR | Strategy-only sideways | async |
| The Retainer | 4 800 EUR/mo | Post-Signature continuation | ongoing |

### Why a middle-tier (Artist Image Sprint 2 400 EUR) is non-optional
- Closes the 7x gap. Now ladder is: 0 -> 890 -> 2400 -> 6500 -> 18000. Ratios: inf / 2.7x / 2.7x / 2.8x. Clean.
- Captures dancers who liked Starter but are NOT ready for 6.5k. Without it, you lose them.
- Different from Starter: more shooting (2 locations or 1 day), 15-20 portraits, 5 videos, full content calendar (30 days), 1 strategy call. Roughly 2x Starter delivery, but priced 2.7x for tier discipline.
- Middle-tier is also what makes Starter feel like an entry — without it, Starter = ceiling.

### Where dancers vs. founders sit
- Dancers/performers: Profile Check -> Starter -> Sprint -> Opening (rare -> Signature only for top earners)
- Founders/doctors/coaches: skip Starter entirely. Enter at Positioning (4.2k) or Opening (6.5k). LTV justifies higher entry; not price-shopping 890 EUR.

**This implies Starter is a wedge for the dancer vertical only.** Do not show it on the founder/doctor landing pages.

## 4. UNIT ECONOMICS — One Signature 18k EUR sale

### Funnel assumptions (conservative-realistic)

Working backwards from one Signature sale:

| Stage | Conv. rate | Volume needed |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Signature client | — | **1** |
| <- Opening upgrade or warm referral | 8% | **12-13 Opening clients** OR equivalent warm pipeline |
| <- Opening from Sprint upgrade | 10% | **120 Sprint clients** OR mix of warm sources |
| <- Sprint from Starter upgrade | 15% | **80 Starter clients** OR warm pipeline |
| <- Starter from Profile Check | 8% | **1000 Profile Checks** |
| <- Profile Check from ManyChat DM | 25% | **4000 DMs** |
| <- DM from SDTV post engagement | 1.0% | **400 000 post views** (engaged) |

**Reality check:** 1000 Profile Checks at 30 min each = 500 hours = 12 weeks of full-time founder labor. **NOT feasible direct path.**

### Realistic path to one Signature

In year 1, **the Signature does NOT come from Starter funnel.** It comes from:
- Direct site traffic (SEO/referral) -> Apply form -> Clarity Call -> Signature
- Founder personal network referrals (Kirill existing network)
- The Starter funnel produces **revenue, testimonials, and cases**, not direct Signature sales.

### What Starter funnel CAN produce in 6 months

Realistic numbers across 6 months of consistent SDTV editorial vertical (10 posts/month):
- ~60 Profile Checks delivered (10/month avg, capped)
- ~6 Starters sold (10% conversion of checks) -> revenue 5 340 EUR (890 x 6)
- ~1 Sprint sold (15% upgrade of Starters) -> +2 400 EUR
- ~0-1 Opening from referral chain -> +0 to 6 500 EUR
- **Total: 7 740 - 14 240 EUR over 6 months from Starter funnel**
- Plus 6-8 testimonials with real photos, which are the actual feedstock for closing higher tiers

### Time cost (Kirill + Wife per week)

During active funnel operation:
- Editorial content production (Wife): 4-6 h/week
- Profile Checks delivery (Kirill, capped at 5/week): 3 h/week
- Starter delivery (Kirill, when one is active): 6-8 h/week
- Strategy/sales (Kirill): 1-2 h/week
- ManyChat/email maintenance (Kirill): 1 h/week
- **Total active: 15-20 h/week founder + 4-6 h/week wife.** That is a 1/3-time job.

Note: Kirill is already at venture #5. This 15-20h/week is real. It comes out of SDTV B2B sales time.

### Break-even analysis

Costs of running the funnel for 6 months (conservative):
- ManyChat Pro: 15 EUR/mo x 6 = 90 EUR
- Resend: 0 (free tier) -> 20 EUR/mo x 6 = 120 EUR if scaled
- Domain + Vercel: 0 (existing)
- Cal.com: 0 (existing)
- Wife SMM time at notional 25 EUR/h x 5h x 26 weeks = 3 250 EUR
- Kirill opportunity cost (foregone SDTV B2B work) at 70 EUR/h x 15h x 26 = 27 300 EUR
- **Total real cost (with founder opportunity cost): ~30 760 EUR**
- **Total cash cost (just expenses + wife): ~3 460 EUR**

**Break-even on cash cost:** 4 Starters sold = 3 560 EUR. Plausible in month 2-3.
**Break-even on opportunity cost:** Need ~35 Starter-equivalents OR 1 Signature. Signature in 6 months from this funnel = unlikely. So opportunity cost is NOT recovered in 6 months.

### Verdict on unit economics
- Funnel is cash-positive quickly, opportunity-cost-negative for 6+ months.
- Justified ONLY if Brandbureau has long-term strategic value beyond cash (testimonials, positioning, future SaaS, etc.). Otherwise, the same 15h/week into SDTV B2B is more productive.
- **Real ROI shows in months 9-18 when warm Sprint -> Opening upgrades start landing.** Not in the first 6 months.

## 5. ALTERNATIVE FUNNELS — 3 non-dancer verticals

Each vertical needs its own engine. Pasting "SDTV -> ManyChat" onto founders/doctors/coaches will not work — wrong audience, wrong trust signal.

### A. Founders (tech, SaaS, fintech)

**Why they buy:** want to be the brand of their company. Investor-readable. Authority in their category.

**Lead sources (ranked):**
1. **LinkedIn outbound (handcrafted, 20/week max).** Target: BCN-based or BCN-friendly founders, post-Series-A, 1k-10k LinkedIn followers. Message angle: "Saw your post about X. Working with founders on visual signature — would you do a 10-min teardown of your current public presence? No pitch." Conversion to call: ~3-5%. Conversion call -> Opening/Signature: ~10-15%. **Realistic: 1-2 deals per quarter from 250 outreach.**
2. **Speaking at BCN founder events (4 Years From Now, Itnig, OnDeck BCN, founder dinners).** Talk topic: "Why your visual brand is leaking 30% of your investor signal." Lead capture via QR -> Cal.com. **Realistic: 1 strong lead per event, 4 events/year = 4 leads, 1-2 conversions.**
3. **Referral partnerships with 2-3 founder-adjacent operators**: a YC mentor, a fintech VC associate, a startup lawyer. Give them a 20% referral fee on Opening+. **Realistic: 1-3 deals per partner per year if relationship is real.**

**Skip:** cold ads, content marketing, SEO. Founders do not read studio blogs. They buy from referrals and recognition.

### B. Doctors / aesthetic medicine / private clinic

**Why they buy:** patient acquisition, premium positioning, escape from hospital-only practice.

**Lead sources (ranked):**
1. **Referrals from existing case (Dr. V. Madrid derm).** This is the ONLY proof-point asset. Get 3-5 video testimonials. Use them in:
   - Direct outreach to other dermatologists/plastic surgeons in BCN/Madrid (LinkedIn + email)
   - Conference visibility (SEME — Spanish Society of Aesthetic Medicine, AEDV — Dermatology)
   - **Realistic: 2-4 deals per year if Dr. V. case is well-packaged.**
2. **Partnership with private clinics or medical equipment distributors.** They sell 30-100k EUR machines to clinics; bundling "we will help you turn this into a personal brand" is high-value. Approach 2-3 distributor reps in Spain. **Realistic: 1 deal per year from this, but if it works it scales.**
3. **Doctolib / TopDoctors profile audits as wedge.** Free audit of public-facing doctor profile (similar to Profile Check, but for medicine). Convert to Positioning or Opening. **Realistic: requires 3 months to build; not v1.**

**Skip:** Instagram funnels (most doctors hate IG self-promo), Meta ads.

### C. Executive / leadership coaches

**Why they buy:** differentiation in a saturated market. Premium positioning to charge 5-15k EUR/coaching engagement instead of 2k.

**Lead sources (ranked):**
1. **EMCC / ICF Spain chapter visibility.** Speak at coach association events. Run "branding for coaches" workshops at 197 EUR — workshop itself is a qualifier funnel. **Realistic: 2-4 conversions per year per workshop run.**
2. **LinkedIn niche-content engagement (NOT cold outreach).** Coaches READ LinkedIn. Comment intelligently on top coach influencers' posts -> eventual DM -> call. Slow but works. Requires Kirill/Wife to invest 2 h/week on LinkedIn engagement. **Realistic: 1 deal per quarter at steady state.**
3. **Coach training program partnerships.** ICF-accredited training programs (CTI, Coaches Training Institute, Hudson) graduate coaches who need to launch their practice. Offer a "graduating coach package" at Sprint price (2 400 EUR) bundled with the program. Approach 2 program directors. **Realistic: high-effort; pursue only if one of the warm referrals lands.**

**Skip:** SDTV cross-promotion (irrelevant audience), generic content marketing.

### Cross-vertical insight

**Brandbureau TRUE moat is photographic taste + Kirill editorial eye, not the funnel.** The funnel is a delivery mechanism. Each vertical needs its own trust signal source. Do not try to unify them into one funnel.

**Recommendation:** v1 (next 6 months) = dancers/performers ONLY. Founders/doctors/coaches stay on the site as positioning, but no active funnel. Pick ONE non-dancer vertical to add in months 7-12 based on which referral lands first.

## 6. BRAND CONFLICT — SDTV vs Brandbureau

### The premium-perception risk is REAL. Here is the precise mechanism:

**SDTV brand association (in target audience minds):**
- Festivals, dance floors, party-vibe, community editorial
- Massive scale (1M+ followers), free content, event coverage
- "Of the people, for the dancers"

**Brandbureau brand association (intended):**
- Premium studio, founder/expert positioning, 18k EUR packages
- Selective, curated, editorial elegance
- "Atelier" — not a service shop

### The conflict is bidirectional

**SDTV -> BB direction:**
- A dancer who comes from SDTV expects SDTV-tier emotional warmth and price (free or low). When they hit "18 000 EUR Signature" they do not feel premium — they feel surprised and locked out.
- For the dancer Starter (890 EUR), the SDTV bridge is fine. For Opening+ tiers, it is a price-shock funnel.

**BB -> SDTV direction (more dangerous):**
- A festival organizer Googling SDTV finds Brandbureau. Wonders: "Are they media or studio? Are they neutral about my talent or do they monetize them on the side?"
- B2B trust takes 2-3 years to build, 1 conversation to crack.
- This is the one Kirill should fear.

### Recommended brand wall

1. **NEVER co-name on public surfaces.** SDTV posts NEVER mention "Brandbureau." Brandbureau site NEVER lists SDTV as a service or partnership.
2. **Editorial vertical inside SDTV stays at SDTV-brand level.** "Artist Image Check by SDTV" is fine. The DM journey routes to brandbureau.studio in PRIVATE only, after a clear off-ramp moment ("for full image rebuild we work with a partner studio in BCN — link below if you want details"). Kirill is the link, not a brand bridge.
3. **Different domains, different palettes, different voice.** Already true (brandbureau.studio vs sdtv.com). Maintain it.
4. **Kirill personal cross-listing is OK.** "Kirill — founder of SDTV (sdtv.com) and Brand Bureau Barcelona (brandbureau.studio)" on his personal LinkedIn. That is the trust bridge — not a co-brand.
5. **Festival organizers ASKING about Brandbureau:** scripted answer. "Brandbureau is my image-direction studio for individual artists and founders — completely separate from SDTV editorial. SDTV is the festival media partner. Different scope, different team, different commercial relationship." Reassures.

### Net assessment

**Public connection = mild brand dilution + moderate B2B risk. Net negative.**
**Private connection (DM-level routing, Kirill personal cross-listing) = slight positive. Net positive.**

Keep it private.

## 7. STOP-RULE — When to kill the funnel

### Hard numerical triggers (any one fires -> 30-day kill review)

Window: 90 days from launch (assume launch = 2026-05-01, audit = 2026-07-30).

**Stop / pivot if at day 90 ANY of:**
1. **<3 paid Starters sold.** Below this, the funnel is theoretical.
2. **<25 Profile Checks delivered.** Below this, demand signal is too weak.
3. **<2.0% click-to-DM conversion on educational SDTV posts.** Below this, the editorial vertical does not work.
4. **Kirill actual hours on Brandbureau >25 h/week sustained for 4+ weeks.** Time is leaking from SDTV/WeDance.
5. **Any organizer mentions SDTV/Brandbureau brand confusion.** Even one — that is the canary.
6. **Wife reports Brandbureau SMM is cannibalizing SDTV SMM time.** Same person, finite hours.

### Soft triggers (audit at 90 days, decision at 180 days)

7. <8 000 EUR total Brandbureau revenue from this funnel by day 180.
8. <1 Sprint or Opening upgrade from the Starter cohort by day 180.
9. No NPS-type signal of "wow" from Starter clients (testimonial fuel is the real product here).

### Pivot options if killed

- **Keep site, kill funnel.** Brandbureau lives passively as an inbound site for direct apply. Zero outbound. Costs 0 EUR/month.
- **Convert to Positioning-only practice.** No shoots. Pure strategy at 4.2k EUR. Higher hourly, lower production overhead.
- **Sell domain + tier system.** It is a clean v1. Could license to another image studio.

### Decision authority on stop

Marco audits at day 90 and day 180. Recommendation goes to Kirill. Kirill decides. Sage to be looped in on day-180 decision (overload signal check).
