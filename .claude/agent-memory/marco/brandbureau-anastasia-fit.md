# Brandbureau — Anastasia Case Fit Audit

**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Author:** Marco
**Question:** Is /work/anastasia (single real case, beauty/editorial model) a credibility asset or a positioning leak for a site that promises founder/doctor/expert/coach/performer work at €6.5k-€18k tiers?

---

## VERDICT (short)

Kirill's instinct is correct. There IS a mismatch — but it is contained, not catastrophic, because Image Studies sits in its own tab. **The real risk is not the photo — it is the spread of the positioning copy on the rest of the site.**

## 1. REAL 3-SECOND RISK FOR A FOUNDER/CEO

A €18k Signature buyer lands on /work, sees one real case = beauty model in editorial dress, eight stub tiles with stock fees. Read in 3 seconds: "this studio shoots models, not operators like me." Bounce probability up. Apply rate down — not collapse, but a measurable softening (estimate 20-30% drop on founder/doctor segment vs a neutral case-less /work). The Image Studies tab partially absorbs this, but most prospects won't read the tab label before forming the impression.

## 2. SPREAD MISMATCH

Site declares 5 verticals. Anastasia covers 0.5 of them (model ≠ performer in the artist sense). Eight stubs cover the other 4 with **fabricated** outcomes. So the proof asset count is: 1 real + 8 fake. **The fake ones are the bigger risk than Anastasia.** A sharp founder smells the "Fee ×1.8" stock tiles instantly. Anastasia at least is real.

Mismatch is not catastrophic, but the site is currently writing checks (5 verticals × premium tiers) that the proof shelf cannot cash.

## 3. VERDICT — OPTIONS

- **A.** Acceptable but lossy — current state leaks founder conversion.
- **B.** Worse than A. Removing the only real asset to keep 8 fakes is going backwards.
- **C.** **Optimal.** Narrow site copy to "performers, artists, image-driven professionals." Keep Anastasia front. Park founders/doctors as "by referral" footnote until real cases land. Honest positioning beats aspirational positioning every time at premium price points.

**Recommendation: C.** Then add founder vertical back when 2 real founder cases exist.

## 4. SANITY CHECK SCENARIO

Kirill shows /work/anastasia to a CEO prospect for €18k Signature. CEO scans, sees beauty editorial, asks: "Do you have a case closer to my situation? CEO, founder, someone running a company?" Kirill says no, only Anastasia. CEO mentally downgrades to "image studio, not a positioning partner for operators." Either ghosts, or negotiates to €6.5k Opening, or asks for 30-50% off Signature. Real money lost: €11k-€18k per encounter.

This is the exact failure mode. It will happen at least once before a founder case lands.

**ACT:** Switch to Option C this week. Founder claim reinstated only when proof exists.
