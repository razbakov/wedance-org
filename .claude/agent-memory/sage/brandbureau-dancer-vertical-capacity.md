## 1. THE REAL QUESTION — Strategic Upgrade or Shiny New Wedge?

**This is wedge-jumping, not strategy.** Calling it directly.

Tells:
- The current MVP (artist Profile Check) launched literally **yesterday** (2026-04-25 sprint kickoff). Zero data yet. Zero paid Starter. The first SDTV post is targeted 2026-05-05, the first 5 Profile Checks aren't even delivered.
- Proposing cohort + membership **before the lower-friction funnel proves demand** is the same pattern Kirill has shown repeatedly: opening venture #2 before #1 is validated. Lumen Atelier → Brand Bureau merge happened because they were the same project (memory log: `feedback_response_format_and_posture` + Maya §15). The ikeegai/wedance/arancha layer all carry this same texture.
- The numbers being floated (€8.9k/cohort × 4-6/year = €35-55k) are seductive but sit in fantasy territory — **no validated demand signal**. Compare to one closed festival deal at €5-15K with proven repeat history.
- The framing "massive emotional-pain segment in SDTV's 1M+ audience" is true but irrelevant to capacity. Pain ≠ paid. Audience ≠ buyers. The 1M followers are organizer-trust-built, not coaching-buyer-trained.

**What's actually happening:** Kirill is excited about a clear product shape (cohort, membership, identity-pain) because it feels more "real" than a 14-day Profile Check delivery. The cohort fantasy is a flight from the slower, less glamorous work of converting the first 5 manual Checks into 1-2 paid Starters.

**Strategic upgrade would look like:** "Profile Check produced 8 paid Starters in 6 weeks, the pain pattern is consistent and identity-coded, now I'm productizing it as a cohort because the 1:1 model is the bottleneck." That conversation is **June at the earliest, not today.**

## 2. CAPACITY IMPACT — Three Scenarios

### Light: identity-language landing as passive inbound (~+2 h/week)

**What:** Add a `/artists/identity` or rewrite hero copy on `/artists` to lean into "stop being invisible" identity language. No cohort, no membership, no live calls. Capture goes into existing Profile Check funnel.

**What breaks first:**
- Nothing structural. The 2 hours come from existing Brand Bureau MVP allocation (8h ceiling per Maya §15).
- Risk: if identity language pulls a different segment than Profile Check format serves, the deliverable mismatches the promise. Profile Check is technical (5-point score, bio, posts). Identity pain is emotional ("I want to be somebody"). The bridge is non-trivial.

**Verdict:** Acceptable IF Kirill writes it himself in 1 sitting and stops there. Not acceptable as a pilot for cohort thinking.

### Medium: launch ONE cohort May-Jun, 6 weeks (~+8-12 h/week active)

**What:** "Stop Being Invisible" cohort, 10 students × €890. Live group calls, content templates, member moderation, 6-week curriculum, delivery infrastructure (Discord/Circle/group calls platform), enrollment funnel.

**What breaks first:**
1. **SPRINT (Apr 22→May 6) — 100% slip.** Sales Layer v1 doesn't ship. Already at 95% risk per current sprint plan; adding cohort prep = certain slip.
2. **SDTV festival pipeline — at least 1 deal lost.** Q2 is critical; cohort design + enrollment marketing eats the same deep-work blocks that festival proposals require. Lost deal cost: €5-15K. Cohort revenue (assuming 100% fill): €8.9K. Negative trade.
3. **Wife — overload.** SMM SOP launch (her sprint goal) gets pushed. Cohort needs visual rhythm + community manager presence + posting cadence change. Asking her now = breaking SOP before release.
4. **Sleep — 3-4 nights/week below 6.5h within 3 weeks.** Cohort enrollment pressure + cohort-week-1 anxiety + festival sales pressure = compound stress. Documented pattern: Kirill drops sleep first when load spikes.
5. **O2 KR3 (2 personal slots/week)** — vanishes by Week 2.

**Verdict:** **Do not run.** This is the scenario where wedge-jumping causes the most damage because it looks doable.

### Heavy: cohort + membership + parallel artist Profile Check (~+15-20 h/week sustained)

**What:** Cohort Apr-Jun + membership €49-99/mo opens parallel + artist Profile Check continues + SDTV editorial vertical 2-3 posts/week.

**What breaks first:**
1. **Kirill — within 4 weeks.** This is not a "what breaks" exercise. This is the exit ramp to clinical burnout. 15-20h/week sustained on cohort+membership ON TOP OF SDTV (25-30h) + festival sales + Brand Bureau MVP + Arancha + ikigai org = 60+ h/week with negative recovery. Sleep gone. Festival pipeline ignored. Wife stress maxed. Kirill loses 2-3 active deals worth €15-30K, then quits the cohort halfway through, refunds 6 students, exits the quarter with less revenue, less health, less trust.
2. **Wife — leaves the room within 2-3 weeks.** Membership management is a job, not a co-pilot task. Asking her to community-moderate while running SMM festivals = relationship damage, not just operational.
3. **SDTV — actual brand damage.** Festival organizers see 2-3 posts/week of dancer-coaching content on SDTV channels. Positioning ("festival media partner") gets blurred. Future organizer pitches harder.
4. **All non-revenue commitments (wedding, health, recovery) — invisible casualties.** Won't even register that they're collapsing until 6 weeks in.

**Verdict:** **Hard no.** This isn't a sustainability question, it's a harm-reduction question.

**You cannot strategically upgrade a funnel that hasn't run.**

The dancer-identity cohort proposal contains 3 hidden assumptions that are unvalidated:

1. **"Insecure dancers want to pay €890 for a 6-week cohort."** Unproven. The Profile Check is meant to test exactly this — willingness-to-pay across the dancer segment. Without that data, the €890 price point is fiction.
2. **"The 1M+ audience contains 40-60 buyers/year for this offer."** Unproven. SDTV audience is built on dance content, not on coaching/identity transformation. Conversion from audience-to-buyer is a separate skill from audience-to-engagement.
3. **"Cohort delivery is a strength."** Unproven. Kirill has never run a cohort. Wife has never run a cohort. The skill stack (curriculum design, group facilitation, member retention, conflict management) is not in the team.

The right way to test these:
- Test #1 in the **next 4 weeks** through Profile Check → Starter conversion data.
- Test #2 in the **next 6-8 weeks** through Starter sale velocity.
- Test #3 in **Q3 at earliest**, after the team has held a 1:few delivery (not 1:many) successfully.

**SDTV festival pipeline is Q2-critical. Real money. Proven product. Established trust. Existing relationships with renewal windows.** Trading Q2 festival hours for unvalidated cohort prep = strategic regression.

The strategic move is: **finish what you started, then strategically upgrade.**
