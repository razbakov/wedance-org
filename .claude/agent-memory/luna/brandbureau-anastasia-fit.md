# Brand Bureau — Anastasia case fit (Luna verdict)

**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Owner:** Luna
**Status:** Honest read for Kirill — voice / audience signal / storytelling

---

## 1. What a visitor actually reads

A founder, doctor, or coach lands on `/work` and sees 8 archetype stubs plus one real face: Anastasia, beauty-editorial, Milan model. The eye doesn't read taxonomy ("Image Study" vs "Case"). The eye reads: *fashion shoot.* Signal received: **"This person is a strong fashion/beauty photographer."** Not "premium image studio that rebuilds my public presence."

A doctor will not project themselves onto Anastasia. A founder will not see "this could be me." The single real reference point on the site pulls the brand toward editorial/modeling — not toward the executive register the copy promises.

## 2. Image Studies vs Cases — does the relabel save it

Partially. The relabel is honest and correct, and I stand by recommending it. But honesty in metadata does not override visual signal. The visitor scrolls a grid. Beauty editorial dominates that grid because it is the only real photography next to stock-driven stubs. The disclaimer protects you legally and ethically. It does not protect positioning.

Type-labels guide *interpretation* once trust is built. They do not *create* trust on first scroll.

## 3. Voice consistency

Conflict, not consistency. Brand Bureau voice on the site: restrained, agency, executive register, "rebuilt around a single editorial portrait." Anastasia case voice (and image): beauty/fashion, model casting, Milan editorial. Two different studios. Same site. The collision is small but real, and a sharp founder will feel it before they can name it.

## 4. Verdict

**Option C — pull Anastasia from `/work` until a real founder/doctor/coach case exists.**

Not for conversion. For brand. The first real case sets the studio's center of gravity. If Anastasia is the only real reference, the studio centers on beauty/editorial — and every founder who lands wonders if they're in the wrong place. Better to ship `/work` with stubs labeled clearly as archetypes than to anchor the brand on a case that pulls audience signal sideways.

Anastasia's narrative belongs in a future `/studio-archive` or `/image-studies` section — separate route, separate intent, no mixing with client work.

## 5. If kept anyway — one rewrite

In the narrative, the line addressed to *"founders, experts, artists, models and performers"* should become: *"This is the work we do for anyone whose public image is part of how they're chosen — founders, doctors, performers, artists."* Lead with the executive registers, demote model. Doesn't fix the visual mismatch, but stops the copy from reinforcing it.
