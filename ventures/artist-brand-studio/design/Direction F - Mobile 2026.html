Six things we don't negotiate.

PRINCIPLE 01
Positioning before production

No frame shot before we agree what it says. If we can't say it in a sentence, the shoot won't rescue it.

PRINCIPLE 02
Fewer, sharper assets

Thirty mediocre pieces make you invisible. Four sharp ones make you legible.

PRINCIPLE 03
Taste is not optional

Every reference, frame and caption is a decision. We don't outsource decisions.

PRINCIPLE 04
The audit tells the truth

If the audit says you're not ready, we say so. We've turned down more than we've taken.

PRINCIPLE 05
Deliverables are a side effect

You're hiring a direction. The files are the evidence.

PRINCIPLE 06
Time is the rarest material

Ninety days, two studio days, six anchor pieces. Scope is a feature.
